## Circuit Network Inspector – Icon Design Brief

Format: 144 × 144 pixels, square app/mod icon.

Core concept: A large magnifying glass dominates the icon, occupying approximately 80% of the available area. It symbolizes inspecting and understanding a Factorio circuit network.

### Composition

- The magnifying glass is the central and dominant element.
- Outside the lens, show two or three circuit wires in red and green.
- One red wire runs continuously through the magnifying glass:
  - Outside the lens, it is thin.
  - Inside the lens, it becomes noticeably thicker, clearly communicating magnification.
- The lens should be transparent enough that the circuit elements underneath remain visible. Keep reflections subtle so they don't obscure the details.

### Circuit elements and signals

Show a decider combinator and a speaker beneath the magnifying glass:

- The decider combinator represents an entity writing to the circuit network. An arrow points from the combinator to the wire.
- The speaker represents an entity reading from the circuit network. An arrow points from the wire to the speaker.
- Both arrows must be clearly visible and distinguishable, even at the small icon size.

Use three recognizable Factorio signal icons:

1. Virtual signal `T`
2. Green checkmark signal
3. Metallic asteroid chunk signal

Important placement: The signal icons may appear on the wire, but they must also be placed directly on or next to the arrows representing the reads and writes. This makes the connection between a signal and an entity's interaction with that signal explicit. They should not simply be decorative symbols scattered across the wire.

### Visual style and constraints

- Recognizably Factorio-inspired, with industrial machinery and circuit-network aesthetics.
- Use authentic-looking Factorio combinator, speaker, wire, and signal designs.
- Strong silhouettes and clear visual hierarchy.
- Keep all important elements legible at 144 × 144 pixels.
- Avoid text, labels, numbers, and unnecessary decorative details.
- The result should read as an application icon, not as a screenshot or a detailed game scene.

### Negative prompt

Avoid oversized signal symbols that obscure the circuit elements, ambiguous arrow directions, disconnected wires, excessive lens reflections, tiny unreadable details, generic electronic components that don't resemble Factorio objects, and any lettering or title text.

The key visual message: The magnifying glass reveals which entities write signals to a circuit network and which entities read them.
